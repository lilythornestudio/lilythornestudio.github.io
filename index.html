Lily Thorne
Woodworking

Lily Thorne is a woodworking artisan in West Stockbridge, MA. She designs and crafts handmade furniture and objects characterised by simple, refined lines assembled using traditional joinery. For each design, she strives to highlight a single focal feature—whether it be an intriguing shape, a contrasting proportion, or a beautifully exposed joint that reveals the craftsmanship behind its creation.
